## Values

# A commitment to putting people and relationships first to drive industry-leading results for founders, employees and investors.

What Makes Black Lake Different

## **Founder-Centric Partnerships**

We respect and augment the original visions of the founders with whom we partner and invest in, including keeping the existing staff, as we create win-win partnerships guided by founder autonomy and empowerment.

## **Experienced Results**

Founders trust us to make things happen, and we have generated extraordinary results. We’ve completed 15 transactions, generating over 10x cash-on-cash returns, with more than $50M going to founders in “encore” transactions.

## **Lean Team Advantage**

Lean team structure empowers us to be nimble, enabling quick decisions and strategic adaptations to meet the unique needs of each business.

## **Tech Industry Focus**

Our deep focus in technology firms allows for an understanding of the unique challenges and opportunities within this sector, allowing us to bring specialized knowledge and extensive expertise to every investment.
